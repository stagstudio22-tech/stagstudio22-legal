Privacy Policy — StagStudio22 Pin Studio

This is a personal, single-user tool used by StagStudio22 to generate and publish Pinterest pins from Amazon product links to my own Pinterest account.

It only accesses the Pinterest account of its owner (StagStudio22) via Pinterest's official API, authorized through Pinterest's OAuth login.
It does not collect, store, or share data belonging to any other user.
Data handled (Amazon product links, generated pin images/text, and a Pinterest access token) is stored locally on the owner's device only and is never transmitted to any third party other than Pinterest and OpenAI (used to generate pin content/images).
No data is sold or shared for advertising purposes.
